Questions and answers

Before you say hello.

What do I need to use WakuWaku?

A Google account and a modern browser. Sign-in connects your daily usage to your verified account.

Where is my conversation history stored?

Your conversation list and messages are stored locally in this browser. Clearing browser data removes them.

How does voice work?

English can use the bundled local Piper model. Japanese, Korean, and other languages use compatible voices available on your device.

Why is there a daily trial limit?

The limit keeps the shared AI service sustainable and is enforced on the server, not only in the interface.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The chat becomes a focused mobile layout with the companion displayed as a video-call-style picture-in-picture view.
